OneBridge Grid — "Span" Language Anatomy

1. Geometric Deck

• No arches or domes anywhere in the product
• Span line: 1.5px hairline with 5px square nodes
• Keystone tag: Verified trapezoid polygon
• Modular grid: 1px Sand hairline borders

2. Radii Tokens

• 6px: Controls (buttons, inputs, select)
• 10px: Panels, cards, and avatar images
• 14px: Modals, slide-overs, bottom sheets
• Rule: Nothing larger, no circular avatars

3. Colour Discipline

• Bridge Orange (#F68713): Fill only, never text
• Charcoal text on orange: Contrast ratio 5.1:1
• Deep Orange (#A04E08): Text/icons on light
• Warm Paper (#FAF7F2): Base canvas

4. Typography & Copy

• Scale: 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 40
• Sentence case: Everywhere in UI
• Monospace nameplate: OB-T15EH7
• Indian format: ₹12,48,000 / +91
