# Presenter script (about 6 minutes)

Audience: investors or customers. Everything shown is the working product with a fictional company, **Sahyadri Steel Works**, a Pune steel supplier with 3 branches and 8 people.

**Before the meeting:** run the 5-minute check in [hosting.md](hosting.md#2-check-it-works-5-minutes-before-every-meeting). Have the dashboard open on a laptop and a phone ready with a QR scanner. Print or display one card QR (Cards → Rohit Deshmukh → Download PNG).

## 1. The problem, in one line (20 seconds)

"When a salesperson leaves, their visiting cards, their contacts and every lead they collected leave with them. OneBridge Grid makes the company own the card, so nothing walks out of the door."

## 2. Admin dashboard journey (2 minutes)

1. **Overview.** "This is the owner's view. Seven of eight cards are live, 120-odd card views, 4 leads this week. The checklist shows what's left to set up."
2. **People.** "Every card sits in a company seat. Here's Rohit, head of sales, at the Pune head office." Open **Manage** on Rohit. "If Rohit leaves, I offboard the seat." Tap **Offboard Rohit Deshmukh**, show the confirmation, then **Cancel**. "His card would point visitors to the company, and his leads stay with the seat for whoever replaces him."
3. **Cards.** Pick Ananya Joshi. Change her job title; the phone preview updates as you type. "What you see on the right is exactly what a customer sees." Tap **Save changes**: "It's live now."
4. **Brand kit.** "Logo and colours apply to every card at once. If someone picks colours that are hard to read, we stop them and suggest a fix." Change the header colour to orange to show the warning, then **Apply recommended text colour**, then **Discard changes**.

## 3. The visitor card on a phone (1.5 minutes)

1. Ask someone to scan the printed QR, or scan it yourself and mirror the phone.
2. "No app needed. One tap saves the contact to their phone, one tap opens WhatsApp with a greeting already typed, and Directions opens Google Maps to the branch."
3. Tap **हिन्दी**: "Hindi at the tap of a button." Tap **EN** to switch back.

## 4. Share-back becomes a lead (1 minute)

1. On the phone: **Share your details back**. Enter a name and number (use a made-up one), tick consent, **Send contact**.
2. "The visitor sees this confirmation." Point at the span line connecting them to the card owner.
3. On the laptop: **Leads**. "And it's already here, against the seat, encrypted at rest. If Rohit leaves tomorrow, this lead still belongs to the company."

## 5. QR codes and standees (1 minute)

1. Cards → Rohit → **QR codes and links**. "Every card gets a QR and a short link."
2. **Add QR for an event or place**, type "Pune builders expo, stall B12". "Each stall or brochure gets its own QR, so you can see which one brought the leads." (Rohit already has one; the demo leads from the expo are tagged with it.)
3. **More formats**: PNG for WhatsApp, SVG for designers, PDF for print.

## 6. Close (20 seconds)

"Company-owned identity, live cards, leads that stay. Step 3 of our build plan is done and this is running on the production code path; next come invites, NFC cards and analytics."

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Page takes long to load | The free plan sleeps; wait 30–60 seconds, or move to a paid plan before meetings |
| Sent back to sign-in after a reload | Tap **Open the demo company** again and carry on; fix the cookie path afterwards (hosting.md step 2) |
| The demo data looks wrong after rehearsals | Reset it (hosting.md section 3), about a minute |
| No network at the venue | Use the screenshots in `docs/demo/screens/` (take them during your check) |

Never type a real person's phone number into the demo.
